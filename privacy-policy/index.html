Immanuel 隐私政策

运营主体：SoulLogic AI LLC。政策地址：immanuel.chat/privacy-policy/。

1. 范围与用途

本政策说明面向美国用户的 Immanuel iOS 应用及后端如何处理信息，用于登录与账户管理、AI 对话和附件理解、圣经阅读、记忆功能、额度与存量订阅核验、防滥用、使用统计及故障诊断。Apple、Google 等第三方服务还受各自政策约束。

2. 处理的信息

3. 第三方处理、地区与模型改进

后端运行在 Google Cloud。Google 的 Firebase Authentication、App Check 和 Google 登录处理登录凭证、应用与设备证明以鉴权及防滥用；Apple 处理登录凭证和存量订阅收据核验；Google Gemini / Vertex AI 处理对话历史、提示词、图片或文档以生成回复；Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics 处理上表中的诊断和使用信息。

目前尚无法确认全部实际启用的其他模型或错误监控提供方、各方处理与存放地区，以及各类数据的具体保留期限。对话会经后端转交第三方处理；不能据此认为只有上述已列服务会接收数据。对于聊天内容是否由我们或第三方用于模型改进、第三方保留和训练条款，目前不能作出已核实的完整说明，也不作“不用于训练”或“零保留”的保证。

4. 保留与删除

  • 聊天、附件、记忆、阅读位置和设置在设备上保存；后端保存账号用量、额度、订阅状态及相关日志。没有可在此承诺的统一保留天数。
  • 在设置中选择“删除账户”并确认后，应用请求后端删除 Firebase 账号、该账号的用量、额度、订阅状态及相关缓存。后端成功后，应用退出登录并清除本机聊天、记忆、附件、阅读位置和伙伴名等数据。删除不可恢复。
  • 设置中可单独删除长期记忆；删除单条消息也会清除与该消息关联的记忆卡片。
  • 删除单条消息或单个会话不会清除相应附件文件。这些附件会保留到删除账户或切换账号时清理。
  • 网络中断会导致删号请求失败，此时本机数据不清除，应用提示错误；本机文件清理失败会在下次登录时重试。
  • 上述后端清理不等于删除 Analytics、Crashlytics、云日志和模型提供方中的全部记录；它们按各自机制处理。我们不能保证逐项即时删除或撤回已经发给模型提供方的内容。
  • 删除 Immanuel 账户不等于取消 Apple 订阅。请在 Apple ID 的订阅设置中自行取消存量订阅；我们不能代为取消，退款须向 Apple 申请。

5. 选择、权利与联系

你可以使用上述账户和记忆删除功能，并在 iOS 设置中关闭相机或相册权限。适用法律可能赋予访问、更正、删除、获取副本等权利；应用内删号并不替代完整的隐私权请求流程。本页目前不能提供已核实可用的公众隐私请求联系人或完整的美国各州权利办理流程。

使用者须年满 13 岁或达到所在地更高的最低同意年龄；未满 18 岁须有监护人许可。这一使用要求不等于已完成年龄核验或儿童隐私合规。

Immanuel Privacy Policy

Operator: SoulLogic AI LLC. Policy address: immanuel.chat/privacy-policy/.

1. Scope and purposes

This policy describes information handling by the Immanuel iOS app and backend, offered to U.S. users, for sign-in and account management, AI conversations and attachment understanding, Bible reading, memory features, allowance and existing subscription verification, abuse prevention, usage statistics and diagnostics. Third-party services, including Apple and Google, also have their own policies.

2. Information processed

The backend runs on Google Cloud. Google's Firebase Authentication, App Check and Google Sign-In process credentials and app and device attestations for authentication and abuse prevention. Apple processes sign-in credentials and existing subscription receipt verification. Google Gemini / Vertex AI processes conversation history, prompts, images or documents to generate replies. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process the usage and diagnostic information above.

We cannot currently confirm the complete list of other active model or error-monitoring providers, their processing and storage locations, or specific retention periods for each data category. Conversations pass through the backend to third parties; do not assume that only the named services receive data. We cannot currently give a complete, verified account of whether we or third parties use chat content for model improvement, or of third-party retention and training terms. We do not promise “no training” or “zero retention.”

4. Retention and deletion

  • Chats, attachments, memory, reading position and settings are stored on your device. The backend stores account usage, allowance, subscription status and related logs. We cannot promise a single retention period here.
  • After you confirm Settings → Delete Account, the App requests deletion of the Firebase account and its backend usage, allowance, subscription state and related cache records. Once the backend succeeds, the App signs you out and clears local chats, memory, attachments, reading position and companion name, among other account data. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Settings has a separate long-term memory deletion control. Deleting a single message also removes memory cards linked to that message.
  • Deleting a message or conversation does not remove its attachment files. Those files remain until cleanup on account deletion or account switching.
  • If the network is unavailable, account deletion fails, local data is not cleared and the App shows an error. Failed local file cleanup is retried at the next sign-in.
  • This backend cleanup does not delete all records in Analytics, Crashlytics, cloud logs or model providers; each system follows its own mechanisms. We cannot guarantee immediate item-by-item deletion or withdrawal of content already sent to model providers.
  • Deleting an Immanuel account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel existing subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings. We cannot cancel them for you; request refunds from Apple.

You can use the account and memory deletion controls above and disable camera or photo-library access in iOS Settings. Applicable law may provide rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information. In-app account deletion is not a complete privacy-rights request process. This page cannot currently provide a verified public privacy-request contact or a complete U.S. state rights request process.

Users must be at least 13, or the higher minimum age of consent where they live; users under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission. This use requirement does not establish age verification or compliance with children's privacy requirements.

6. Security and changes

App requests carry Firebase identity and App Check tokens for verification. No system is perfectly secure. This page does not promise a specific retention period, change-notification channel or advance-notice period; updates do not override applicable legal requirements.
